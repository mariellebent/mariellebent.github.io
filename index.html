Enter the Godwoven World

A realm shaped by gods, ether, and secrets buried for centuries

The Gods

Immortal. Powerful. And not always benevolent.

The gods have shaped the world through their rivalries, bargains, and ambitions. Some are worshipped while others are feared. But none have truly abandoned the mortal realm.

Their influence lingers in ancient prophecies, forbidden magic, and the fates of those fortunate (or unfortunate) enough to draw their attention.

✦Discover the gods

Etherals and Daemons

Two divine creations. Two opposing purposes.

Created by the gods, Etherals are magical beings said to bring balance, protection, and prosperity to the mortal world. From majestic dragons to elusive faeries, their existence is a testament to the gods' power.

But not all divine creations were intended for good. Daemons, born of the god of death, were created to spread darkness, destruction, and suffering.

☾Learn about ether

The Empire

Ether is forbidden. A dragon guards the throne.

For centuries, the Empire has stood beneath the rule of an immortal emperor and the watchful eye of his dragon.

Ether is outlawed, rebellion is ruthlessly suppressed, and the crown's authority reaches across the realm. Yet beneath the Empire's carefully maintained order, ancient secrets linger, and whispers of resistance threaten to unravel everything.

Meet the Author

Marielle A. Bent

Marielle Bent lives in Wisconsin with her husband, their daughter, a boisterous dog and a grumpy cat. She began writing fantasy after a lifelong love of stories about dragons, gods, and unlikely heroes. Her work draws inspiration from the epic adventures she grew up reading, blending classic fantasy elements with darker mythology and character-driven storytelling. She is deeply fascinated by characters who carry both deep empathy and the capacity for darkness. This became the foundation of The Godwoven Trilogy.
