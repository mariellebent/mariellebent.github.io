Meet the Characters

The people caught in a world of forbidden magic, old gods, and dangerous secrets

The Godwoven Trilogy

Saber

Feared by the living. Haunted by the dead.

Known as the vigilante Nightstalker, Saber has spent her life concealing a dangerous secret. Cursed by the god of death, she is forced to relive the final memories of murder victims, witnessing their deaths through their own eyes.

Feared by many and trusted by few, she finds an unlikely companion in Firefly, a mischievous miniature dragon with a fierce loyalty that far exceeds his size.

When a string of mysterious murders draws her into the King of Thieves’ world of secrets and political intrigue, Saber discovers that the dead may hold the key to a conspiracy reaching far beyond the Empire’s throne.

Her Story

  • A curse bestowed by the god of death
  • An unlikely friendship with a miniature dragon
  • A deadly mystery and forbidden magic

The Godwoven Trilogy

Rynn

The King of Thieves. A ruler without a crown.

From the lawless streets of Dravenport, Rynn commands an empire built on secrets, stolen fortunes, and carefully earned loyalties.

Charming when it suits him and ruthless when necessary, he has carved out a kingdom beyond the Emperor's reach, where thieves, smugglers, and outcasts have found a place to call home.

When his friend is killed, Rynn seeks the help of a mysterious woman named Saber, whose unusual abilities may hold the key to uncovering the killer. But what begins as a search for answers soon draws them both into a web of dangerous secrets, political intrigue, and a conspiracy reaching far beyond a single murder.

His Story

  • A cunning ruler of thieves, smugglers, and outcasts
  • An unlikely alliance with Saber
  • A dangerous web of secrets, betrayal, and political intrigue
